12/31週四 Thu

抵達宿霧，Plaza Independencia 跨年煙火Arrive in Cebu, New Year's Eve fireworks at Plaza Independencia

  1. 上午Morning
    抵達麥克坦－宿霧國際機場Arrive at Mactan-Cebu International Airport

    入境時出示兩人的 eTravel QR code、回程機票與住宿確認。Show both eTravel QR codes, return tickets and hotel confirmations at immigration.

  2. 接著Then
    搭 Grab 到 IT ParkGrab to IT Park

    車程約 45–75 分鐘，車資約 ₱600–900。Grab 上車點在第一、第二航廈入境大廳對面的停車場。About 45–75 min, ₱600–900. Grab pickup is in the car park opposite Terminal 1 and 2 arrivals.

  3. 中午Noon
    Seda Central Bloc 寄放行李Drop bags at Seda Central Bloc

  4. 17:30
    晚餐（備選）：House of LechonDinner (option): House of Lechon

    宿霧烤乳豬名店，在 Acacia St，離飯店約 1.5 公里，Grab 約 10 分鐘。每天 10:00–21:00，人均 ₱500–1,000。跨年夜人多，12 月中先訂位（autoreserve.com 或電話 +63 916 662 0163）。Cebu's famous lechon spot on Acacia St, about 1.5 km from the hotel (10 min by Grab). Open 10:00–21:00, ₱500–1,000 per person. Book by mid-December via autoreserve.com or +63 916 662 0163.

  5. 20:30
    前往 Plaza IndependenciaHead to Plaza Independencia

    市中心的歷史廣場，旁邊就是聖佩德羅堡和 1/1 要搭船的 Pier 1，離飯店約 4 公里，平常車程 20–30 分鐘。廣場周邊會交通管制，Grab 只能送到管制區外，下車後走路進場。今年交管路線 12 月公布Road closures announced in DecemberA historic square downtown, next to Fort San Pedro and Pier 1 (your ferry terminal on 1/1). About 4 km from the hotel, normally 20–30 min. Roads around the plaza are closed, so Grab drops you outside the zone and you walk in.

  6. 00:00
    市府官方跨年倒數與煙火Official city countdown and fireworks

    Plaza Independencia 是宿霧市政府的官方跨年會場，2024、2025 年都在這裡倒數，同時是 Sinulog 祭典的開幕煙火。去年 17:00 就開始表演節目。2026 年細節 12 月公布2026 details in DecemberPlaza Independencia hosts Cebu City's official countdown, held here in both 2024 and 2025, which also opens the Sinulog festival. Last year the stage program started at 17:00.

  7. 午夜後After midnight
    回飯店Back to the hotel

    散場時 Grab 很難叫、會加價。先往外走出管制區、離開人潮，再叫車；最穩的做法是請飯店櫃台安排來回包車，先約好接送地點。Rides are scarce and surge-priced when the crowd leaves. Walk out of the closed zone first, then book a Grab. The safest option is a return car arranged by the hotel, with a pickup point agreed in advance.

領隊叮嚀Tour leader notes
  • 早班機起得早，下午務必補眠，晚上才撐得到跨年。After the early flight, nap in the afternoon so you can stay up for midnight.
  • 去年會場周邊從 12/31 下午 3 點管制到 1/1 清晨 5 點。晚上 9 點前到，比較容易找到好位置，也避開最塞的時段。Last year roads around the plaza closed from 3 pm on 12/31 to 5 am on 1/1. Arriving before 9 pm makes it easier to find a good spot and beat the worst traffic.
  • 只帶手機、少量現金和一張卡，背包背在前面；大型活動通常會檢查包包，不要帶大包。Bring only your phone, a little cash and one card, and wear your bag in front. Large events usually check bags, so leave big bags at the hotel.
  • 兩人先約好走散時的集合點，例如聖佩德羅堡正門。人多時手機訊號可能變差。Agree on a meeting point in case you get separated, such as the main gate of Fort San Pedro. Mobile signal can drop in big crowds.
  • 帶水、穿好走的鞋。街上常有民間放鞭炮和煙火，保持距離。Bring water and wear comfortable shoes. Expect private firecrackers on the streets; keep your distance.
  • 睡前先把隔天搭船的行李整理好。Pack for the next day's ferry before bed.

搭船到薄荷島Ferry to Bohol

  1. 上午Morning
    退房，搭 Grab 到 Pier 1Check out, Grab to Pier 1

    元旦早上路上車少，但碼頭人多，提早出門。Roads are quiet on New Year's morning, but the pier is busy. Leave early.

  2. 開船前Before sailing
    碼頭報到Check in at the pier

    開船前 45–60 分鐘到，報到截止是開船前 30 分鐘。碼頭稅要另外用披索現金付。Arrive 45–60 min early; check-in closes 30 min before departure. The terminal fee is paid separately in pesos, cash only.

  3. 11:40
    OceanJet 商務艙 → 塔比拉蘭OceanJet Business Class → Tagbilaran

    航程約 2 小時，候選班次 11:40、13:00、14:00。船票尚未開放Not on sale yetAbout 2 hours. Candidate sailings: 11:40, 13:00, 14:00.

  4. 下午Afternoon
    港口接送到邦勞島Transfer to Panglao

    車程 30–45 分鐘。薄荷島沒有 Grab，請飯店接送或預約包車。30–45 min. There is no Grab on Bohol, so book a hotel transfer or private van.

  5. 順路On the way
    Common Crew 咖啡（可選）Common Crew Coffee (optional)

    自家烘焙的咖啡館，就在港口往飯店的路上，離飯店約 2 公里。營業 10:00–18:00，週三公休。包車的話先跟司機說要停。元旦是否營業待確認New Year's Day hours to confirmA specialty roaster on the road from the port, about 2 km before the resort. Open 10:00–18:00, closed Wednesdays. Tell the driver in advance if you want to stop.

  6. 15:00
    Henann Tawala Resort 入住Check in at Henann Tawala Resort

    15:00 起可入住。入住時要付押金，每晚 ₱3,000。Check-in from 15:00. A deposit of ₱3,000 per night is required.

  7. 傍晚Sunset
    Alona Beach 看夕陽Sunset at Alona Beach

    從飯店走路約 2 分鐘。About a 2-minute walk from the resort.

  8. 晚上Dinner
    Alona 一帶晚餐（步行 5–10 分鐘）Dinner around Alona (5–10 min walk)

    海鮮：Jumbo Crab Bohol、No.1 Seafood Restaurant；披薩：A Tavola、Toto e Peppino；平價烤雞：The Original Paeng's。飯後到 Mocobay 吃冰淇淋或芒果冰沙。Seafood: Jumbo Crab Bohol or No.1 Seafood Restaurant. Pizza: A Tavola or Toto e Peppino. Budget grilled chicken: The Original Paeng's. Gelato or mango shake at Mocobay afterwards.

領隊叮嚀Tour leader notes
  • 商務艙每人可免費託運 20 公斤，手提限 10 公斤、25×35×40 公分；有輪子的行李箱一律要託運。Business Class allows 20 kg checked per person; carry-on up to 10 kg and 25×35×40 cm. Wheeled suitcases must be checked.
  • 容易暈船的話，上船前 30 分鐘吃暈船藥。If you get seasick, take medication 30 minutes before boarding.
  • 颱風或強風警報時船班可能停駛，出發前一天先看海況和 OceanJet 公告。Sailings may be cancelled during storm warnings. Check sea conditions and OceanJet notices the day before.
  • 元旦假期人多，海鮮餐廳先訂位；螃蟹和海鮮多半秤重計價，點餐前先問清楚價錢。It's a holiday, so book the seafood restaurants. Crab and seafood are usually priced by weight; ask before you order.

1/2週六 Sat

包車半日遊：巧克力山、眼鏡猴，下午按摩Private van: Chocolate Hills and tarsiers, massage in the afternoon

  1. 07:30
    包車從飯店出發Private van from the resort

    私人包車約每車 ₱2,500–4,000，依時數計價。12–2 月是旺季，提前 2–3 週預約。待預約To bookAbout ₱2,500–4,000 per van, depending on hours. December to February is peak season; book 2–3 weeks ahead.

  2. 09:30
    巧克力山Chocolate Hills

    先去最遠的點，車程約 2 小時。門票 ₱100，觀景台要爬 200 多階，停留約 1 小時。早上人少、光線好。Go to the farthest stop first, about 2 hours' drive. Entry ₱100; the viewing deck is up 200-plus steps. Allow about an hour. Fewer crowds and better light in the morning.

  3. 11:30
    菲律賓眼鏡猴保護區（Corella）Philippine Tarsier Sanctuary (Corella)

    回程順路，車程約 1 小時。開放 08:30–15:30，門票 ₱60–100，停留約 45 分鐘。On the way back, about an hour's drive. Open 08:30–15:30, entry ₱60–100, about 45 minutes.

  4. 中午Lunch
    路上午餐Lunch on the road

    請司機推薦沿路餐廳，或撐到回邦勞島再吃。Ask the driver for a place along the way, or wait until you're back on Panglao.

  5. 14:30
    回飯店，下午按摩Back to the resort, massage in the afternoon

    都在 Alona 一帶，走路 10 分鐘內：Luna Tiger Spa（13:00–23:00）、궁스파 Goong spa 全身按摩（11:00–23:30）、ALONA ZEN SPA（11:00–23:00）、Goong foot spa 腳底按摩（11:30–23:30）。All around Alona, within a 10-minute walk: Luna Tiger Spa (13:00–23:00), Goong Spa body massage (11:00–23:30), Alona Zen Spa (11:00–23:00), Goong Foot Spa (11:30–23:30).

  6. 晚上Dinner
    晚餐Dinner

    走路可到：Giuseppe Pizzeria（約 900 公尺）、BARWOO（約 1 公里，21:00 打烊）。搭三輪車 5–10 分鐘：MIST、6+ RESTAURANT、MOSA、Kamaya Restaurant & Café。Walkable: Giuseppe Pizzeria (about 900 m) or BARWOO (about 1 km, closes 21:00). By tricycle, 5–10 min: MIST, 6+ Restaurant, MOSA or Kamaya Restaurant & Café.

領隊叮嚀Tour leader notes
  • 跟司機講明要去 Corella 的 Philippine Tarsier Sanctuary。路上也有其他眼鏡猴參觀點，環境和保育方式不一樣。Tell the driver you want the Philippine Tarsier Sanctuary in Corella. Other tarsier stops along the road are run differently.
  • 眼鏡猴白天在睡覺，保持安靜、不用閃光燈、不要觸摸。Tarsiers sleep during the day. Keep quiet, no flash, no touching.
  • 1 月是雨季尾聲，巧克力山多半還是綠色，乾季（3–5 月）才會轉成咖啡色。January is the end of the rainy season, so the hills are usually still green. They turn brown in the dry season (March to May).
  • 鄉間景點只收現金，準備小額披索。包車價格先講好包含幾小時、油錢與過路費，司機小費約 ₱200–300。Countryside stops are cash only; bring small pesos. Agree on hours, fuel and tolls in the van price. Tip the driver about ₱200–300.
  • 熱門按摩店下午到晚上常客滿，前一天先打電話預約。晚上回飯店的路燈少、路窄，遠一點的餐廳搭三輪車，上車前先談好價錢。Popular spas fill up in the afternoon and evening, so call a day ahead. Roads are narrow and dark at night; take a tricycle to farther restaurants and agree on the fare first.
  • 雨天備案：上午留在飯店泳池，下午按摩，晚上去 Bohol Bee Farm 吃東西。Rain plan: pool in the morning, massage in the afternoon, dinner at Bohol Bee Farm.
