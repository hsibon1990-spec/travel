12/31週四 Thu

抵達宿霧，在 IT Park 跨年Arrive in Cebu, New Year's Eve at IT Park

  1. 上午Morning
    抵達麥克坦－宿霧國際機場Arrive at Mactan-Cebu International Airport

    入境時出示兩人的 eTravel QR code、回程機票與住宿確認。Show both eTravel QR codes, return tickets and hotel confirmations at immigration.

  2. 接著Then
    搭 Grab 到 IT ParkGrab to IT Park

    車程約 45–75 分鐘，車資約 ₱600–900。Grab 上車點在第一、第二航廈入境大廳對面的停車場。About 45–75 min, ₱600–900. Grab pickup is in the car park opposite Terminal 1 and 2 arrivals.

  3. 中午Noon
    Seda Central Bloc 寄放行李Drop bags at Seda Central Bloc

    先放行李、在 Ayala Malls Central Bloc 吃午餐，下午回飯店入住補眠。Leave bags, lunch at Ayala Malls Central Bloc, then check in and nap in the afternoon.

  4. 晚上Evening
    跨年晚餐與煙火New Year's Eve dinner and fireworks

    IT Park 一帶的高樓餐廳有跨年方案，可以邊吃邊看煙火。今年方案 12 月公布Announced in DecemberRooftop restaurants around IT Park offer countdown packages with fireworks views.

領隊叮嚀Tour leader notes
  • 早班機起得早，下午務必補眠，晚上才撐得到跨年。After the early flight, nap in the afternoon so you can stay up for midnight.
  • 跨年夜街上常有民間放鞭炮和煙火，保持距離，貴重物品收好。Expect private firecrackers on the streets. Keep your distance and keep valuables secure.
  • 午夜後很難叫車，住 IT Park 走路就能回飯店，這是選這間的好處。Rides are hard to get after midnight. Staying in IT Park means you can walk back.
  • 睡前先把隔天搭船的行李整理好。Pack for the next day's ferry before bed.
住宿 Stay：Seda Central Bloc Cebu
1/1週五・元旦 Fri, New Year's Day

搭船到薄荷島Ferry to Bohol

  1. 上午Morning
    退房，搭 Grab 到 Pier 1Check out, Grab to Pier 1

    元旦早上路上車少，但碼頭人多，提早出門。Roads are quiet on New Year's morning, but the pier is busy. Leave early.

  2. 開船前Before sailing
    碼頭報到Check in at the pier

    開船前 45–60 分鐘到，報到截止是開船前 30 分鐘。碼頭稅要另外用披索現金付。Arrive 45–60 min early; check-in closes 30 min before departure. The terminal fee is paid separately in pesos, cash only.

  3. 11:40
    OceanJet 商務艙 → 塔比拉蘭OceanJet Business Class → Tagbilaran

    航程約 2 小時，候選班次 11:40、13:00、14:00。船票尚未開放Not on sale yetAbout 2 hours. Candidate sailings: 11:40, 13:00, 14:00.

  4. 下午Afternoon
    港口接送到邦勞島Transfer to Panglao

    車程 30–45 分鐘。薄荷島沒有 Grab，請飯店接送或預約包車。30–45 min. There is no Grab on Bohol, so book a hotel transfer or private van.

  5. 15:00
    Henann Tawala Resort 入住Check in at Henann Tawala Resort

    15:00 起可入住。入住時要付押金，每晚 ₱3,000。Check-in from 15:00. A deposit of ₱3,000 per night is required.

  6. 傍晚Sunset
    Alona Beach 看夕陽Sunset at Alona Beach

    從飯店走路約 2 分鐘。About a 2-minute walk from the resort.

領隊叮嚀Tour leader notes
  • 商務艙每人可免費託運 20 公斤，手提限 10 公斤、25×35×40 公分；有輪子的行李箱一律要託運。Business Class allows 20 kg checked per person; carry-on up to 10 kg and 25×35×40 cm. Wheeled suitcases must be checked.
  • 容易暈船的話，上船前 30 分鐘吃暈船藥。If you get seasick, take medication 30 minutes before boarding.
  • 颱風或強風警報時船班可能停駛，出發前一天先看海況和 OceanJet 公告。Sailings may be cancelled during storm warnings. Check sea conditions and OceanJet notices the day before.
住宿 Stay：Henann Tawala Resort
1/2週六 Sat

薄荷島一日遊（建議路線）Bohol countryside tour (suggested)

  1. 07:30
    包車從飯店出發Private van from the resort

    私人包車約每車 ₱2,500–4,000。12–2 月是旺季，建議提前 2–3 週預約。待確認To confirmAbout ₱2,500–4,000 per van. December to February is peak season; book 2–3 weeks ahead.

  2. 上午Morning
    巧克力山Chocolate Hills

    門票 ₱100，停留約 1 小時。早上人少、光線也好。Entry ₱100, about 1 hour. Fewer crowds and better light in the morning.

  3. 接著Then
    眼鏡猴保護區Tarsier Sanctuary

    門票 ₱60–100，停留約 45 分鐘。Entry ₱60–100, about 45 minutes.

  4. 中午Lunch
    羅柏河竹筏自助午餐Loboc River cruise lunch

    每人 ₱850–950，約 1.5 小時，船上有樂手表演。₱850–950 per person, about 1.5 hours, with live music on board.

  5. 下午Afternoon
    人造森林、Baclayon 教堂Man-made Forest, Baclayon Church

    森林拍照約 15 分鐘；教堂免費參觀，可隨意奉獻。About 15 minutes for photos in the forest. The church is free; donations welcome.

  6. 傍晚Evening
    回飯店休息Back to the resort

    全程約 8–9 小時，晚餐在飯店或 Alona Beach 一帶。The full day takes 8–9 hours. Dinner at the resort or around Alona Beach.

領隊叮嚀Tour leader notes
  • 鄉間景點多半只收現金，準備小額披索，遊河樂手小費約 ₱50–100。Countryside stops are mostly cash only. Bring small pesos; tip the river cruise musicians about ₱50–100.
  • 眼鏡猴白天在睡覺，保持安靜、不用閃光燈。Tarsiers sleep during the day. Keep quiet and don't use flash.
  • 雨天備案：留在飯店泳池放鬆，下午去 Bohol Bee Farm 吃東西。Rain plan: relax at the resort pool, then visit Bohol Bee Farm in the afternoon.
住宿 Stay：Henann Tawala Resort

    行前準備Before you go

    出發前 72 小時內Within 72 hours of arrival

    • 兩人各自上 etravel.gov.ph 登錄 eTravel（免費），截圖存好 QR code。Each traveler registers on etravel.gov.ph (free) and saves the QR code.
    • 確認船票、飯店接送、包車都已訂好。Confirm ferry tickets, hotel transfers and the private van.

    證件與文件Documents

    • 護照：入境日起效期 6 個月以上。Passport valid for at least 6 months from arrival.
    • 回程機票、兩間飯店的訂房確認，入境時可能會被要求出示。Return ticket and both hotel confirmations; immigration may ask for them.
    • 旅遊平安險與不便險的保單資訊。Travel insurance details.

    行李清單Packing list

    • 夏季衣物、薄外套（冷氣強）、泳衣、拖鞋、好走的鞋。Summer clothes, a light jacket for strong air-con, swimwear, sandals, walking shoes.
    • 防曬乳、帽子、防蚊液、暈船藥、常備藥。Sunscreen, hat, insect repellent, seasickness pills, personal medicine.
    • 萬用轉接頭：菲律賓是 220V，A、B、C 型插頭。Travel adapter: 220V, plug types A, B and C.
    • 小額披索現金：碼頭稅、三輪車、小費、鄉間景點門票。Small pesos in cash for terminal fees, tricycles, tips and countryside entry fees.

    交通Getting around

    宿霧 Cebu

    Grab 叫車Grab

    市區移動以 Grab 為主，雨天和尖峰時段會加價。宿霧沒有捷運，BRT 公車目前只有部分路段營運（SRP 到 IT Park）。Grab is the main way around the city; fares rise in rain and rush hour. Cebu has no metro; the BRT runs partially between SRP and IT Park.

    機場 ↔ IT Park Airport ↔ IT Park

    約 45–75 分鐘About 45–75 minutes

    Grab 約 ₱600–900；黃色跳表計程車約 ₱500–700，要確認司機有跳表。Grab ₱600–900; yellow metered taxi ₱500–700. Make sure the meter is on.

    薄荷島 Bohol

    沒有 GrabNo Grab

    港口與飯店之間請飯店接送，或事先預約包車。三輪車（tricycle）適合短程，上車前先談好價錢。Use a hotel transfer or a pre-booked van between the port and the resort. Tricycles are fine for short trips; agree on the price first.

    宿霧 ↔ 薄荷島 Cebu ↔ Bohol

    OceanJet 快船OceanJet fast ferry

    • 宿霧 Pier 1 到塔比拉蘭約 2 小時，每天約 16 班。Cebu Pier 1 to Tagbilaran takes about 2 hours, around 16 sailings a day.
    • 開船前 45–60 分鐘到碼頭，報到截止開船前 30 分鐘。Arrive 45–60 minutes early; check-in closes 30 minutes before departure.
    • 碼頭稅用披索現金另外付。The terminal fee is paid separately in cash.

    IT Park

    Ayala Malls Central Bloc

    就在住宿旁邊，走路就到，吃飯逛街都方便。Right next to the hotel, an easy walk for meals and shopping.

    宿霧老牌大型購物中心，品牌多、餐廳多。Cebu's long-established big mall with many brands and restaurants.

    伴手禮Souvenirs

    芒果乾是最常見的伴手禮，各大超市都買得到。Dried mangoes are the classic gift and are sold in every supermarket.

    餐廳Food

    宿霧 Cebu

    烤乳豬 lechonLechon (roast pig)

    宿霧的招牌菜，皮脆肉香。Rico's Lechon、Zubuchon 都是當地常見的名店。Cebu's signature dish with crispy skin. Rico's Lechon and Zubuchon are well-known local spots.

    邦勞島 Panglao

    Bohol Bee Farm

    邦勞島上的有機農場餐廳，冰淇淋和麵包很有名。An organic farm restaurant on Panglao, known for its ice cream and bread.

    薄荷島 Bohol

    羅柏河竹筏午餐Loboc River cruise lunch

    坐在竹筏上沿河吃自助午餐，是一日遊的固定行程。A buffet lunch on a floating raft, a standard stop on the countryside tour.

    用餐提醒Dining tips

    • 不生飲自來水，喝瓶裝水。Don't drink tap water; drink bottled water.
    • 帳單常已含服務費，沒含的話可以留約一成小費。Bills often include a service charge; if not, a tip of about 10% is appreciated.

    景點Sights

    薄荷島 Bohol

    巧克力山Chocolate Hills

    上千座圓錐形小山，乾季草色轉褐，看起來像一顆顆巧克力。Over a thousand cone-shaped hills that turn brown in the dry season, like rows of chocolate drops.

    薄荷島 Bohol

    眼鏡猴保護區Tarsier Sanctuary

    世界最小的靈長類之一。白天在睡覺，請保持安靜、不用閃光燈。One of the world's smallest primates. They sleep by day, so keep quiet and don't use flash.

    邦勞島 Panglao

    Alona Beach

    從飯店走路就到的白沙灘，傍晚看夕陽。A white-sand beach a short walk from the resort; best at sunset.

    宿霧 Cebu

    聖嬰大教堂、麥哲倫十字架Basilica del Santo Niño, Magellan's Cross

    宿霧最有代表性的歷史景點，兩處就在隔壁。Cebu's best-known historic sites, right next to each other.

    有趣的事Fun facts

    Sinulog 祭典Sinulog Festival

    宿霧每年 1 月第三個星期日舉辦大遊行，跨年夜常常一起辦開幕煙火。Cebu's grand parade is held on the third Sunday of January; the New Year's Eve fireworks often double as its opening.

    吉普尼 jeepneyJeepneys

    彩繪得很誇張的改裝吉普車，是菲律賓最常見的大眾交通。Brightly painted converted jeeps, the most common public transport in the Philippines.

    宿霧是菲律賓最古老的城市The oldest city in the Philippines

    1521 年麥哲倫在這裡登陸，市區還留著西班牙時期的建築。Magellan landed here in 1521, and Spanish-era buildings still stand in the city.
